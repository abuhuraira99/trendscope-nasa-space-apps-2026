# Music

The soundtrack is original. It is synthesised from scratch by `scripts/make-music.mjs` every time
the video is rendered: additive sine pads on a slow chord progression (Dmaj9, Bm11, Gmaj9, Em9,
Dmaj9, Dadd9), a soft sub bass, a few quiet bell tones and a small Freeverb-style reverb. No
samples, loops, recordings or third-party tracks are used, so there is no source page or licence
to credit and no copyright restriction on it.

- Source: `video/scripts/make-music.mjs` in this repository (generated at render time)
- Licence: original work by Team TrendScope, released with the repository under the MIT licence
- Length: 29 s, 48 kHz stereo, 1.4 s fade in, 2.6 s fade out
- Level: peaks at -9 dBFS, about -23 dBFS RMS, so it sits low under the picture
